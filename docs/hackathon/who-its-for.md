# Who GrowthReceipt is for

Pricing is planned and not shown in the app. GrowthReceipt charges nothing today.

## The person

GrowthReceipt is for indie founders and small teams selling digital products. They market their own products, post on LinkedIn or X, and take payments through PayPal.

Typical products:

* A small SaaS tool or scanner, like LaunchCheck in the demo
* A template, course or digital pack
* An app with a paid upgrade sold on the web

## The issue they have today

They can see likes, views and clicks for each post. They cannot see which post brought the people who paid. So the next post is a guess based on engagement, and engagement often points the wrong way. A post with many likes can bring no buyers while a quiet one brings three.

Ad platforms and full attribution suites solve this for teams with ad budgets and tracking setups. A maker posting organically has neither.

## What GrowthReceipt gives them

* Posts drafted from a short brief or a link to the product page, approved by the maker before anything goes out
* One buy link per post, so each PayPal purchase is tied to the post that brought the customer
* Revenue per post that only counts payments PayPal confirmed
* One next experiment, chosen from those verified purchases and approved by the maker, with a plain label for how strong the evidence is

## Who it is not for yet

* Teams running paid ads who already have pixel based attribution
* Stores whose checkout lives on Shopify or another platform GrowthReceipt does not read
* Anyone who needs payments outside PayPal

## Planned pricing

| Plan | Price | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Free, plus 3% of verified revenue collected through PayPal | 1 product, 1 active campaign, 5 agent runs a month |
| Maker | $19 a month or $190 a year, no revenue fee | 5 products, unlimited campaigns, 50 agent runs a month, LinkedIn publishing |
| Team | $49 a month or $490 a year, no revenue fee | 3 seats, 15 products, 200 agent runs a month, shared approvals |

Why this shape:

* Starter costs nothing until the maker earns. The fee is a share of revenue GrowthReceipt helped prove, so the price follows the value.
* The fee is collected as a PayPal platform fee on each payment (PayPal multiparty payments), so the maker never gets a separate bill.
* Once a maker earns more than about $630 a month through campaigns, Maker at $19 costs less than the 3% fee, so growing makers have a clear reason to upgrade.
* Annual plans are priced at 10 months, a common two months free offer.

Dependency: the 3% platform fee needs PayPal partner approval for multiparty payments, which can take weeks. Until then the hosted demo runs on one PayPal sandbox merchant and takes no platform fee.
